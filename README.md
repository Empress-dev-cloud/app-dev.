# Favorite Series: Food Wars

**Food Wars** The story centers on Soma Yukihira, a determined teenager who works alongside his father, Joichiro, at their small family diner, Restaurant Yukihira. Soma's lifelong goal is to surpass his father in culinary skill and take over the diner. However, Joichiro abruptly closes the restaurant to travel the world and challenges Soma to survive and graduate from Totsuki Saryo Culinary Institute—an ultra-elite culinary academy in Japan where fewer than 10% of students graduate.

## Why It Stands Out
* **The Shokugeki System: Formal cooking battles governed by strict rules. Both parties must agree on a theme ingredient or dish, an equal stake, and an odd-numbered panel of judges.

## Main character Highlights
1. **Soma Yukihira** – The protagonist—a resourceful, fearless diner chef aiming to surpass his father and prove that everyday, working-class cooking can defeat fine-dining elitism.
2. **Erina Nakiri** – The "God Tongue", granddaughter of Totsuki's director, and the youngest member of the Council of Ten. Initially arrogant and dismissive of Soma's background, she serves as his primary rival before gradually becoming his closest ally and central focus of the later arcs.
3. **Megumi Tadokoro** – Soma's closest friend and Polar Star Dormitory housemate. Originally anxious and on the verge of expulsion, she blossoms into a formidable chef specializing in comforting, regional Japanese home cooking and hospitality.
